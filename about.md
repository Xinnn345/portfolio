# About Me

Hello! I'm Lily, an aspiring data professional passionate
about using data to understand problems, discover patterns,
and support evidence-based decisions.

## My Background

I am developing my knowledge in data analytics, statistical
analysis, data visualization, and machine learning through
academic projects and practical experience.

I enjoy exploring datasets, identifying meaningful trends,
and presenting findings in a way that is understandable
to both technical and non-technical audiences.

## My Interests

- Data Analytics and Business Intelligence
- Data Cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data Visualization and Dashboard Development
- Natural Language Processing and Sentiment Analysis
- Machine Learning and Predictive Analytics

## My Career Goals

I aim to build a career where I can combine analytical
thinking, technical knowledge, and business understanding
to help organizations make informed decisions.

I am particularly interested in opportunities where I can
continue learning, work with real-world datasets, and
contribute to data-driven projects.
